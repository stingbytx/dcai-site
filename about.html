About dotcomAI

Making Powerful AI
Accessible to Everyone.

dotcomAI is a Sri Lankan-built AI platform created with one simple mission: to make powerful artificial intelligence accessible and affordable for everyone, especially Sri Lankan students.

Our Mission

One Platform. Hundreds of AI Models.

Today, some of the world's most capable AI models can be expensive or difficult to access. We believe that the cost of technology should not prevent talented students, developers, researchers, creators, businesses, and individuals from turning their ideas into reality.

That's why we built dotcomAI. Instead of being limited to a single AI model, dotcomAI brings 400+ AI models from 50+ providers together in one platform. Users can choose the AI model that best fits their task and switch between models instantly within the same conversation.

Whether you need help with programming, research, writing, creative work, analysis, brainstorming, or everyday tasks, dotcomAI gives you access to a broad ecosystem of AI models from one place. Our platform includes models from leading AI ecosystems such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, xAI, Qwen, Z.ai, Meta, Mistral, NVIDIA, and many others.

Why We Created dotcomAI

We Saw a Gap.

There are incredibly talented people in Sri Lanka with the skills, creativity, and ambition to build amazing things—but access to powerful AI technology can be expensive. For many students in particular, paying for multiple AI subscriptions simply isn't realistic.

We wanted to change that. dotcomAI was created to give people a place where they can access a wide range of powerful AI models without needing to subscribe to multiple platforms.

Our goal is not just to provide AI. Our goal is to remove barriers between people and the technology they need to create, learn, build, and innovate.

More Than Just One AI

Freedom to Choose from Hundreds.

Platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini primarily center their experience around their respective AI ecosystems. dotcomAI takes a different approach.

Instead of asking you to choose one AI platform, we give you the ability to choose from hundreds of AI models in one place.

You don't have to stay with the same model for every task. You can start a conversation with one model and switch to another model when you need a different capability—all within the same chat. This gives users the freedom to experiment, compare, and choose the AI that works best for them.

Conversations That Remember

dotcomAI maintains chat context and memory, so you can continue your work without constantly repeating important information.

Affordable AI Access

dotcomAI offers both Free and Paid plans, with the paid plan priced at just LKR 1,000 per month — designed around accessibility for Sri Lankan users.

Built in Sri Lanka 🇱🇰

We believe Sri Lanka has a huge amount of untapped technological talent — students, developers, researchers, creators, and entrepreneurs with the potential to build globally meaningful products.
